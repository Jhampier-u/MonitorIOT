We want to monitor an older adult who lives alone. In the bedroom there is a temperature sensor and in the kitchen a carbon monoxide and smoke sensor; both take readings every 10 minutes. A Raspberry Pi 3 reads these sensors via Bluetooth and sends the data to a local server in the house through a REST service. In addition, the person wears a smartwatch that measures their heart rate every 30 minutes and sends it to the local server through an MQTT broker. Every day, the local server calculates the average, maximum and minimum heart rate and stores that summary in a database on Google Cloud.
